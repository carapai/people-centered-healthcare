# Current state

Last updated: 2026-11-22.

## Phase

Phase 0 (research and Wayfinder) is complete. Implementation is well past half way: 132 of 161 tasks are done, including every task of Phases 2, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9 and 13 is done, and the counts per phase are in [progress](progress.md). Each task gets its own PR against `main`.

The Studio starts from what is designed (#855): its home has a card per kind of design (forms, workflows, concepts & value sets, decision tables, programs, age band sets, indicators, reports; dashboards coming soon) and Packages, each section lists its designs across packages from `GET /api/studio/designs` with where each stands, *New* creates one in a draft it starts or reuses, and every editor's header names its package and offers *Edit*, which starts or reuses the person's draft. The Studio (Phase 11) has started: the package list and the read-only draft page (#492) are built at central, with each package's allowed actions derived on the server from the lifecycle's rules. A draft is edited in the browser one file at a time (#494): each save carries the draft's revision as `If-Match`, so a stale tab gets 412 and overwrites nothing; a draft starts blank or as a copy of a published version at the next minor `-draft` version, may change version until submission, and *Check now* stores validation and simulation results without moving it. Each draft has one Draft Holder (#493), who alone edits, checks, submits, hands over or withdraws it: a handover takes effect only when accepted, `metadata.manage` reassigns with a reason kept in the history and the audit, everyone who has held a draft is one of its authors for the separation of duties, and a withdrawn draft is kept with its history while its `name@version` can be drafted again. Each draft has one discussion (#496): the history the lifecycle writes, with the comments anyone in the Studio adds among it, each optionally naming one of the draft's artefacts, which the draft page links to its editor; a comment is a lifecycle record, audited and never removed, and moves neither the stage nor the revision. Language Packs (#502) are a package kind with one approval: their entries record the English they translate, and central reports which are stale or gone against the target; the merge when served and the translation editor come later. Override points (#507) are declared by base packages with a class each; override packages are checked against them at publication, and a base version that orphans or narrows below a scope's value cannot be activated there. The Facility settings page and the self-approved Apply path come later. Package Validation now checks each profile's snapshot against its differential (#520), so a stale or hand-edited snapshot, or a differential-only profile, cannot be published. A draft's Questionnaire opens in the form designer (#497, part 1): its questions by Form Layout section, an inspector that keeps the Question, its Layout and what it becomes apart, a live preview in the real renderer and the problems found while editing, saved with the Form Layout through the same `If-Match` path; a question published in the previous version keeps its id. Part 2 adds *Becomes a record*, each question's records read from and written to the package's Extraction Mapping (resource type and code, an Observation added or removed), and importing a pasted Questionnaire, R4 converted to R5 with a conversion report in which anything unconverted blocks the import. Central does the reading and the conversion, in `health-fhir-mapping` (`POST /api/studio/packages/{id}/questionnaires/import`, #853); the client holds no R4 code, which `check-boundaries` enforces. Part 3 binds a choice question to a ValueSet through the terminology picker (published ValueSets and same-package drafts, a code preview, the suggested widget, `url|version`), and the preview offers the bound codes. Conditions other than "is Yes" are not designed yet (studio.md). The terminology browser (#523) opens a draft's CodeSystem or ValueSet in three columns: the code systems it is authored against (local or external, with version and concept count), a code system's hierarchy and search by code or words run at central a page at a time, and the open ValueSet's expansion with its change against the published one (removals flagged) and the forms that bind it; a concept is added alone, with everything under it (an `is-a` filter) or excluded. Its ValueSet search and expansion are what the form designer's picker will use. A draft's local CodeSystem is edited in the same browser (#524): *Add a code* and *+ under* in the tree, the concept panel as a form (code, display, definition, parent), each edit saved on its own at central under the same `If-Match` path. A published code is fixed: it can be retired (kept with `status` `retired`, so records still resolve, and left out of expansions for new forms) but not deleted, renamed or moved; a new one can. The CodeSystem takes the version its change calls for against the previous published one (adding or retiring a code minor, a display a patch, redefining or removing a code major), and beside the tree *Version* says why and *What it reaches* lists each ValueSet drawing on it with the codes it gains or loses and the forms that bind it. An optional note goes to the draft's discussion, naming the CodeSystem. External code systems stay read only. A draft's Workflow Definition opens in the Workflow Studio (#498, part 1): the states as a tree with each state's exits, the statechart on an `@xyflow/react` canvas laid out left to right by dagre (compound and parallel states as groups; dragging from one state to another adds a transition), and an inspector for a state (id, fixed once published; kind; parent; initial child; wait, human task, payment gate or sub-workflow; entry actions; its exits, with *Add a transition*) or a transition (source, target, event, timer or eventless, FHIRPath guard with the declared data it may read, its competitors in document order, raise and assign actions). The publication checks the browser can run are listed above it and rerun on every edit, each selecting its element and outlined in red; it saves the Library through the same `If-Match` path. Part 2 picks typed actions from the release's catalogue, which central serves (`GET /api/studio/workflow-actions`, the catalogue publication checks against; no context declares an action yet), with their permission, compensation and parameter fields generated from their schemas; checks their references and parameters; and declares a permission the workflow principal lacks for a timer or eventless step from that problem. The decision-table editor (#516) opens a definition's tables from the Workflow Studio: named rules in order with *fires* and *never fires* markers, each rule's all / any / not tree over the declared data requirements saved as one FHIRPath expression, and central's checks (gaps without a default, rules that never fire, failing test cases) and *Try a patient* on the draft table; *Save as test* writes a `decision-table-test` into `tests/`, which submission's simulation runs. Decision-table guards on transitions wait on #846. The Workflow Studio's *Sandbox* mode (#499) plays the draft, unsaved edits included, in the engine's simulation at central with every action stubbed (`POST /api/studio/packages/{id}/workflows/sandbox`): the browser keeps only the author's guard answers, gate outcomes and moves, and central replays them from the start and answers every step (configuration, stubbed actions, open tasks, pending timers, the events accepted), so undo and restart are just fewer moves and the client never decides a transition. A move needing an unanswered guard is reported and not taken. The active states are lit on the canvas and in the outline; *Save as scenario* writes the run as a `workflow-scenario` into `tests/`, each step expecting what it showed, and it passes submission's simulation unchanged. Opening a saved scenario in the sandbox is not designed yet. *What changed* names, under each changed artefact, the element paths that changed against the latest published version, each with a one-line summary and the bump it needs (#495), and the Draft Holder may add one change note for the whole draft. A program package's ProgramPlanDefinition opens in the Program Designer (#500): a strip with its eligibility, care workflow, scopes and override points; the journey board, one column per state of its care workflow with each stage's forms, what happens, investigations, regimen templates (ordered, first match wins, each citing its protocol, reordered with up and down and saved with `If-Match`), schedule, outcomes and indicators and its exits; and the package panel with the files by kind and *Still missing*. Every linked artefact is in this draft, published or missing, and opens in its editor, or *Create* adds it to the draft and opens it. Publication refuses a regimen template without its condition, its ActivityDefinition or an approved protocol source. Walking a sample patient through is #501. Indicators have started (#548): a count indicator (a `cohort` Measure with its required period date, basis and `initial-population` FHIRPath criteria) is evaluated over one period of any type, counting people once by when care happened (`health-analytics`, `health-analytics-app`); Validation refuses a Measure without a period date, and `POST /api/studio/packages/{id}/indicators/try` evaluates a draft Measure over the package's `test-patient` files and answers the value and why each patient counts or not. What an indicator calculates (#549): a count, a percentage, a rate (its factor in `indicator-rate-per`), a ratio, or a sum, average, median, minimum, maximum or distinct count of a value (a `measure-observation` with its `aggregateMethod`, its value an expression or the time between two dates, each person's or enrollment's first or last), counting people, enrollments, visits or observations, cumulative to the period's end if asked, with the numerator always within the denominator; each summary carries what roll-up needs (count, denominator, the values' count, sum, minimum and maximum), and `Summary::roll_up` recomputes from summed parts, refusing medians and distinct counts. Criteria read a patient's other records through `%records`. Date and membership conditions (#550): criteria compare a declared date with `%periodStart` and `%periodEnd` (on, before, after, between, within N days, weeks or months) or with `%previousPeriods.start` (in the previous N periods of the Report's type), and *is in* another indicator's numerator or denominator, pinned to `url|version` (`'<url>|<version>' in %indicators.numerator`), which evaluation (and *Try*, from the package's own Measures) computes first; Validation refuses an unpinned reference and indicators that refer to each other in a circle. The condition tree that writes and reads them for the editor is `features/studio/indicators/conditions.ts`, on the decision tables' tree. The ANC reference package holds *ANC 1st visits*, *ANC 1st visit before 12 weeks*, the median gestational age at the first visit, *ANC visits* and *ANC 1st visits with the next visit booked*, with eleven test patients, enrollments, gestational ages and booked next visits. Age Band Sets (#551) are `Library` resources of type `age-band-set`: MoH's ANC and outpatient sets are in the national core reference package `ug.moh.core`, Validation refuses a gap or an overlap between bands, a changed band is a major version and a rename a patch, and a Measure pins a set by `url|version` (`indicator-age-band-set` on a stratifier) and keeps that version's bands, from every published version (`Publications::age_band_sets`). The Studio's *Age band sets* section lists them, and each opens as a small table (name, unit, the date age is taken at, from–to rows) with gaps and overlaps flagged on the rows and the set's next version. Population Estimate denominators wait for Population packages (ADR 0071). Disaggregations (#552): an indicator lists, on its first group's stratifiers, an Age Band Set pinned to `url|version` or any coded value (a FHIRPath expression named by the stratifier's code), each with a *not recorded* band (no birth date, an age in no band, no code), so the bands add up to the total; each counted thing falls in the band of its first record in the denominator, so a percentage per band uses the band's own denominator, and the summary carries each band's parts, which roll up like the whole. *ANC 1st visits* is disaggregated by MoH's ANC age bands, and *Try* answers the figure per band and each record's band, taking pinned sets from every published version and the draft's own. The indicator editor (#537) opens a Measure from the *Indicators* section, the draft page or the Program Designer: the definition on the left (name, records read, what is counted, the required period date, the calculation with its rate factor, value and first or last, cumulative, the numerator and the denominator behind *+ Add denominator* / *Remove denominator*, each an all / any / not tree with *+ condition*, *+ date*, *+ in another indicator*, *+ group* and raw FHIRPath, shown compiled, and disaggregations by a pinned Age Band Set or a coded value), problems as Validation would refuse them (no name or period date, a reference to itself, unpinned or unknown, a circle), and *Try* on the right by period type and period, with the figure, each band and every test patient's records and reasons; *Save as test* writes an `indicator-test` that submission's simulation evaluates again. The local backend seeds the ANC reference package as the author's draft. Reports (#556) live in Reporting packages, a package kind with one approval, at `configuration/reports/<id>.json`: a title, one period type, the levels it runs for, and table sections (indicator rows and Age Band Sets pinned to `url|version`, coded disaggregations on rows or columns, totals, % change, *send to DHIS2*) or line list sections (one row per enrollment or visit, attribute and concept columns); Validation refuses medians, distinct counts and line lists in a Report that runs above facility level. The reference package `ug.moh.hmis` holds the HMIS 105 antenatal care Report; the local backend drafts `ug.moh.hmis`. The Report designer (#559) opens a Report from the *Reports* section (*New report* creates one in a Reporting package) or the draft page: the outline (settings, sections as an accordion, one open at a time, each table's disaggregations on rows or columns, totals, % change, *send to DHIS2* and indicator rows, each line list's rows, program, visit types, *only those counted by* and columns), Validation beside it as the author edits, the Report computed for a period and level over test patients (`POST …/reports/preview`; clicking a cell opens its section and says who it counted), and the cells of sent sections listed for the DHIS2 mapping sheet. Line list rows and crossed cells are not previewed yet. DHIS2 cell mappings are #560, running a Report #557, and a Population Estimate denominator waits for Population packages. Each Facility Edge Node computes its facility's indicators from its change log, offline (#554): a changed patient is evaluated again alone, their membership kept per indicator and period they have care in, and each changed period's summary `MeasureReport` calculated again from every patient's membership and stored per indicator, period and facility, for the active Reports' period types and the standard weeks, months, quarters and financial years; a visit entered in April for a March date changes March. Everything is computed again daily, when the active packages change, or after a restore, and that agrees with the incremental path. The summaries sync to central (#555): each sync round sends `/sync/v1/indicator-summaries` the summaries whose figures central does not hold and the removals of those forgotten since, central refuses any that is another facility's or holds anything that could name a patient, keeps them per facility with each facility's last sync, and rolls an indicator up over a given set of facilities from summed parts (`health_analytics_app::roll_up`), a facility that has not synced since the period began shown as missing, never as zero.
